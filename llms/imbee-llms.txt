# imBee

> imBee is an AI-powered Business Omnichannel Messaging Platform headquartered in Hong Kong. We unify all customer messaging channels — WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WeChat, LINE, SMS — into one enterprise-grade inbox with AI automation and ISO 27001-certified security. We serve 10,000+ enterprises across 60+ industries in Asia-Pacific, with deep expertise in Financial Services, Healthcare, Hospitality, Logistics, Property Management, and Retail.

## Core products

- [Omnichannel Inbox](https://www.imbee.io/omnichannel-inbox): Unified inbox consolidating WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WeChat, LINE, SMS and other channels. Supports team assignment, tagging, productivity analytics, and internal team chat.
- [Marketing Campaigns & Broadcast](https://www.imbee.io/marketing-campaigns): Bulk messaging across WhatsApp Business API, SMS, and other channels with auto-handlers, dynamic contact groups, broadcast analytics, and coupon distribution.
- [Chatbot & Automation](https://www.imbee.io/chatbot-automation): Workflow automation with 300+ integrations, including Shopify, HubSpot, Salesforce, Stripe, and Okta SSO.
- [AiskBee — Generative AI](https://www.imbee.io/generative-ai-aiskbee): ChatGPT-powered enterprise AI assistant trained on each company's own knowledge base. Drafts agent responses and personalizes upselling.
- [AI Agent](https://www.imbee.io/ai-agent): Autonomous AI agent for handling customer conversations end-to-end at scale.
- [AI Ambassador](https://www.imbee.io/ai-ambassador): AI-powered conversational engagement for marketing and brand presence.
- [Data Governance](https://www.imbee.io/data-governance): ISO 27001-certified data security, compliance, audit trails, and access controls. Designed for Financial Services and Healthcare requirements.

- ISO 27001 certified from day one — not a roadmap item.
- Headquartered in Hong Kong, supporting clients across HK, TW, SG, MY, ID, TH, PH, VN.
